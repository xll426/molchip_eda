5.3    Distinguishing Operational Validity from
       Optimization Effectiveness
The error analysis in Figure 3 reveals a practical mismatch between
open-ended code generation and state-constrained EDA execution.
A Tcl command may be syntactically correct yet invalid for the
active tool version, design state, or flow stage, causing an agent
to fail before its optimization strategy is exercised. Benchmarks
should therefore distinguish operational validity—whether actions
are legal and state-appropriate—from optimization effectiveness—
